## Frozen Bottle

### What I worked on

At Frozen Bottle, influencer marketing was closely tied to the brand's monthly campaigns, offers, and product moments.

I worked with creators across food, lifestyle, and local categories, managing collaborations both in-house and through **7 influencer marketing agencies**. The focus was not just on getting creators onboard, but on finding the right fit for each campaign and turning creator content into reach and brand discovery.

### What we achieved

- **48M+ campaign views** across influencer campaigns
- **13.6M views** - Great Indian Summer Carnival
- **11.3M views** - Milkshake & Sundae Carnival
- **9M views** - Milkshake & Sundae Carnival
- **6.7M views** - Milkshake Carnival
- **4.7M views** - Flat 50% Off campaign
- **3M views** - Get Anything @99 campaign
- Multiple creator reels crossed **1M+ views**
- Supported Instagram growth from **24K → 60K followers**

## Kongsi Tea Bar

### What I worked on

For Kongsi Tea Bar, I managed influencer marketing **in-house**, focusing on local creators across **Bangalore and Surat**.

I worked with **43 influencers** across food, lifestyle, and local creator categories. Creator selection was based on audience relevance, engagement, and expected cost efficiency rather than just follower count.

The campaigns were built around brand positioning, seasonal launches, and the **Kongsi Tea Bar × Holiday Inn** launch.

### What we achieved

- **43 influencers** managed
- **1.32M+ views** generated
- **11.6K+ likes**
- **900+ comments**
- **14.8K+ shares**
- **₹1.37L** total campaign spend
- **₹0.104 average CPV**
